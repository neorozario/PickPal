# PickPal - Your Decision Making Friend

A modern, minimalist web application that helps you make decisions with style. Whether you're deciding what to eat, need a yes/no answer, or want to make a custom decision, PickPal is here to help!

## 🌟 Features

- **Food Suggestions**: Get random food recommendations from various cuisines:
  - South Indian
  - North Indian
  - Chinese
  - American
  - Fast Food

- **Decision Tools**:
  - Yes/No/Maybe answers
  - Coin flip
  - Dice roll
  - Custom decision maker (add your own options)

- **Modern Design**:
  - Clean, minimalist interface
  - Smooth animations
  - Responsive layout
  - Elegant color scheme
  - Interactive elements

## 🎨 Design

PickPal features a sophisticated color palette:
- Primary: #EEE5DA (warm off-white)
- Secondary: #262424 (dark gray)
- Accent: #b3a9a0 (muted accent)

## 🚀 Usage

1. **Food Suggestions**:
   - Click "What Should I Eat?"
   - Get a random food suggestion with its category

2. **Yes/No Decisions**:
   - Click "Yes or No?"
   - Receive a definitive answer

3. **Maybe Decisions**:
   - Click "Maybe?"
   - Get a nuanced response

4. **Coin Flip**:
   - Click "Flip a Coin"
   - Get Heads or Tails

5. **Dice Roll**:
   - Click "Roll a Die"
   - Get a random number from 1-6

6. **Custom Decisions**:
   - Click "Custom Decision"
   - Enter your options separated by commas
   - Press Enter to get a random selection
